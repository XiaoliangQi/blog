This is the first post in the new blog.

The shape is intentionally simple: a short index, a wide reading column, and a comment thread beside each essay. The writing should be the loudest thing on the page.

## What belongs here

I want this space to be useful for ideas that are still becoming precise. Some posts may be polished essays. Some may be field notes, reading responses, or sketches that need public pressure before they become anything more formal.

That means the design has to leave room for uncertainty. It should feel calm enough for long paragraphs, but alive enough that a reader can respond.

## A practical note

Posts are Markdown files in this repository. Add a new file under `content/posts`, give it a title, date, and summary in the front matter, and the blog will include it automatically.
