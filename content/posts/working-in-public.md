There is a useful difference between publishing early and pretending to be done.

The former says: here is the current state of the thought, with its scaffolding still visible. The latter says: here is a conclusion, please do not ask how it got here.

## The value of a visible trail

When a thought is still forming, readers can sometimes see what the author cannot. They notice an unexamined assumption, a sharper analogy, or a missing counterexample. Comments are not just applause or correction; at their best, they are part of the thinking environment.

That is the spirit of this blog. It is a place to make the trail visible.
